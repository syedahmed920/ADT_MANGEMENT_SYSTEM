Full Workflow Side-by-Side Comparison

Current State

Ongoing Relocation

  1. Application & Payment: After applying Relocation and payment, the workflow drops at Development bucket for ADT assigning.
  2. Field Link Deployment: Access field team deploys link through given ADT and updates back office.
  3. Back Office Closure: Access back office team runs closure as per details given by field team.
Recommended Workflow

Recommended Relocation

  1. ADT Assignment: After signup and payment workflow at Development for ADT assigning.
  2. Proximity Search: MW automatically shows customer nearest all ADT.
  3. Port Configuration: Need to add option add ADT port in MW which is mandatory to run closures.
  4. Field Link Deployment: Access field team deployed link from given or nearest ADT and update to back office regarding ADT change with ADT port.
  5. Back Office Execution: Access back office team runs closures with verified port details.
