Interim Solution Workflows

Interim Solution:

Deployment (Interim)

  • Temporary workaround workflow for assigning and managing ADT deployments.
  • Field team coordination with manual or existing tracking mechanisms.
  • Back-office closure validation while full integrations are pending.

Outdoor Relocation (Interim)

  • Interim relocation application tracking and assignment process.
  • Verification of existing port availability and physical link reassignment.
  • Temporary closure procedure before automated GIS/MW integration.

Migration (Interim)

  • Interim protocol for migrating customers between ADTs.
  • Coordination between field engineers and NOC team for port adjustments.
  • Tracking port statuses manually until automated NMS release is active.

Termination (Interim)

  • Device return logging and store acknowledgment process.
  • Interim workflow to track freed ports and notify technical teams.
  • Manual port clearance tracking pending automated NMS release.
